# 0.15.6 VideoSessionRecovery — real-device acceptance

## TARGET

At the macOS LoginWindow, the first pre-login video connection must either deliver the first H.264 frame promptly or fail with a concrete native-capture diagnostic. A stale prior video generation must never block replacements for 20 seconds or create an endless retry loop.

## Required checks

1. Reboot the Mac to LoginWindow and connect from Zorin.
2. Confirm Desktop and Video authentication succeed.
3. Confirm one of these happens within a few seconds:
   - `First H.264 frame decoded: ...` and the login screen becomes visible, or
   - a concrete `no first H.264 access unit within 6s; native=...` error identifies the native capture stage that failed.
4. Disconnect/reconnect video several times and confirm there is no `previous video session did not stop within 20s` loop.
5. While a video session is intentionally stalled, start a replacement and confirm the old video socket is actively shut down and the replacement proceeds within the bounded replacement grace.
6. Confirm the Desktop/control channel remains connected while video is retried.
7. After the first visible LoginWindow frame, verify input and normal Aqua handoff separately.

## Failure conditions

- `waiting for first frame` persists indefinitely.
- Any replacement waits 20 seconds behind a stale video owner.
- Video retries accumulate stale native capture generations.
- The control/Desktop channel is torn down only because video startup failed.
